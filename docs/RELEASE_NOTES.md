**HTML-Viewer.ipa** is unsigned. Sign it with your own signing service or tool before installing; the
[README](https://github.com/varnealt2-cloud/html-viewer-ios#install) has step-by-step instructions with and without a computer.

- Works on iPhone and iPad, iOS / iPadOS 15 or later.
- Open one file with a single tap, several files together, or a whole folder.
- Tabs, history and recent pages are restored when you reopen the app.
